6.3 Daily Automation Workflow

  1. Open VS Code → Launch Claude Code
  2. Prompt: "Generate this week's content for Edummu Trekkers based on the content calendar. Use my brand voice."
  3. Claude reads memory.md + templates → Generates all posts
  4. Review generated content (optional edit)
  5. Prompt: "Create visuals and schedule everything per the calendar"
  6. Claude uses Blotato MCP → Schedules all posts
  7. Verify in Blotato dashboard → Done!
Time Required Per Week (After Setup): 30-60 minutes

7. Brand Voice Configuration

The following will be saved in memory.md for persistent brand identity:

# Edummu Trekkers - Brand Voice ## Tone - Adventurous, warm, and grounded - Inspirational but not preachy - Friendly like a fellow trekker, not corporate ## Audience - Age 22-45, urban professionals seeking adventure - First-time and experienced trekkers - Nature lovers interested in Northeast India - Budget-conscious adventure seekers ## Language Style - Mix of English and occasional Hindi phrases - Use trekking terminology naturally - Short, punchy sentences for Instagram/Twitter - Longer storytelling for LinkedIn/Facebook ## Do's - Share real stories and experiences - Highlight Arunachal Pradesh's unique beauty - Include practical tips and info - Use nature emojis sparingly (mountain, sunrise, boots, tent) - Tag locations when relevant ## Don'ts - Don't use overly formal language - Don't oversell or use pushy sales language - Don't post without context or storytelling - Don't use more than 3-4 emojis per post - Don't ignore environmental messaging ## Hashtag Strategy Primary: #EdummuTrekkers #ArunachalTrekking #NortheastIndia Secondary: #TrekkingIndia #AdventureTravel #HimalayanTreks Seasonal: (varies by trek/season)
